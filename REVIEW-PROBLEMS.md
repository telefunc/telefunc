# REVIEW-PROBLEMS — Problem-Variability Audit (Reactive Queries)

Two independent design-optimality audits (Opus xhigh, gpt-5.6-sol xhigh; 2026-07-14) of the reactive-queries final plan, adjudicated by the orchestrator. Criterion per problem: **10 = solved in an obviously optimal way; 0 = highly unclear whether a better solution exists.** Full audit texts: epic artifacts `reactive-queries/optimality-audit/` (Opus) and the Sol audit recorded in the debate thread. Grounding: live SOTA survey — Convex, Zero/Rocicorp, Materialize, Feldera, Readyset, ElectricSQL, Supabase Realtime, Hasura, pg_ivm, Debezium, PG docs, TanStack DB.

Scores below: `O` = Opus, `S` = Sol, `→` = adjudicated outcome. Alternatives listed only where they changed (or nearly changed) the decision — the audits enumerate the full sets.

## Net outcome

- **Switched:** CDC topology (single-reader relay over the shared Broadcast path, per-instance as opt-in), PositionedChangeSource contract (positions/gaps/resync as contract law), semantic-environment identity + RLS coarse-gating, schema-epoch retirement, two-level plan/instance identity, telemetry contract, ORM per-writer seq gap-detection, invariant 1/2 rewording (honest per-source delivery scoping; ≥-transaction atomicity).
- **Rejected with analysis:** commit-boundary unfolding (sound for invalidation semantics; mandatory in Phase 2), stateful-join deferral (user mandate + over-fire severity), keep-warm TTL, libpg_query switch (recorded as spike), MySQL binlog/SQLite outbox in P1 (defined as future sources).
- **Averages:** Opus median 8 (no 10s, "strong"; 3 rows actionable); Sol mean 6.0 (5 must-switch, 4 recommended — all either adopted, partially adopted, or rejected with reasoning above).

The compiler core (partial-compilation soundness, CDC retraction, race fence, security authority, verification) scored 8–9 from both auditors independently — the design's center of gravity is sound. The switches concentrate where the plan met production topology (slots, delivery guarantees, DDL, RLS) — exactly where fresh SOTA grounding beat debate-internal reasoning.
