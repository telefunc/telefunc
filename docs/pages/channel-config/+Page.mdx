**Environment**: server.

<TelefuncStreamBeta />

```ts
// Environment: server

import { config } from 'telefunc'

config.channel = {
  reconnectTimeout: 60_000,      // How long a client may be gone and get its channels back (ms)
  pingInterval: 5_000,           // How often a client pings; it counts as gone after twice this (ms)
  maxPendingBytes: 268_435_456,  // The most one channel holds for a client not taking it (bytes)
}
```

> **You rarely need any of these.** `await` your `send()`s and a channel holds one message at a time, whatever the client does.

> Each setting decides one thing, and none of them can break another:
> - `reconnectTimeout`: a client that comes back within it finds its channels open, with nothing lost. Past it, they close with `NetworkError`. Raise it for clients on bad networks. Lower it to free a gone client's channels sooner.
> - `pingInterval`: how fast a client that stopped answering counts as gone, which starts its `reconnectTimeout`. Raise it to save a phone's battery. Lower it to notice sooner.
> - `maxPendingBytes`: only `send()`s nobody awaits reach it. Past it, a `send()` rejects with `ChannelOverflowError` and that message isn't delivered, and the channel stays open. A larger message is refused even if you await it. Lower it to use less memory, which below 64 MiB also limits how much a channel sends per round trip, in both directions.

> These apply to each channel, not to a connection or a server: 100 channels may hold 100 × `maxPendingBytes` in total. On Cloudflare, `maxPendingBytes` defaults to 32 MiB, as <Link href="/stream/cloudflare">a Durable Object's memory</Link> is limited to 128 MB.

> Telefunc refuses a value that can't work when you assign it: `reconnectTimeout` or `pingInterval` under 1 second, or `maxPendingBytes` under 2 MiB. Assign `config.channel` before your server handles its first request: once a channel exists, a change is refused, as open channels would keep the old values.

> This is the **server-side** `config.channel`, a separate object from the **client-side** <Link href="/transport"><code>config.channel.transports</code></Link> (`telefunc/client`).
